# PART 1 — Top 5 Rules Everyone Asks About

## 1. Death Jester's "Gamble any number of times" — when do you actually cash out?

**← lead here: the single most-argued rule in the game.**

- **Short answer:** All gambling happens **before** the Action resolves. Keep gambling as long as you like;
  stop whenever you want. **One failed Gamble and everything is lost** — all previous successes, the Action
  itself, no effect — and you can't gamble again. If you stop on a success, resolve the Action **X+1 times**
  (X = successful Gambles), and only **then** resolve "on a successful Gamble" effects, X times.
  `[FAQ p.2]`, matching `[RB p.29]`
- **Why people get it wrong:** the card says *"Repeat this Action for each successful Gamble"* — and the
  Action itself begins *"Before this Action, you may Gamble…"*. So players read each repeat as granting a
  **fresh** gamble opportunity, which chains into an infinite loop. It doesn't: the repeats are damage only,
  the gambling window already closed.
- **Receipts:** 80 posts across 3 threads — 34p ([3618776](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3618776)),
  25p/1r ([3633347](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3633347)),
  21p ([3620227](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3620227)).
- **The kicker:** a player found an **infinite-damage loop** with the Jester's unlockable *Marotte* weapon +
  the *Empress* Evolution card. The publisher answered it on Discord tagged `#FAQsneakPeak` — and that answer
  shipped nearly **verbatim** as the official FAQ entry. The forum literally wrote the FAQ.
- **Clip caption:** *"One bad gamble doesn't cost you the bonus — it costs you the whole turn."*

## 2. Do you have to take exactly two actions — or can you take one and stop?

- **Short answer:** You take **exactly 2 Actions** per Turn — but **Pass is itself an Action**. So "1 action,
  then Pass" is legal and is how you end your involvement in the phase. `[RB p.11]`
- **Why people get it wrong:** the rulebook states "exactly 2 Actions" and lists Pass separately, so players
  conclude that if you can only afford one Action you're stuck. The tutorial booklet settles it with a worked
  example of two hunters each taking one die-Action and then a Pass.
- **Receipts:** 10p / **4 recommends** — the highest-recommended thread in the whole Rules forum —
  [3611560](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3611560).
- **Remember:** passing also discards **all** your remaining active dice, and you're skipped for the rest of
  the phase. It's a real cost, not a free out.
- **Clip caption:** *"You're not trapped. Passing IS your second action."*

## 3. Can you attack the "Next Turn" card — and when does the track actually refill?

- **Short answer:** **Yes, the *Next Turn* card is a legal target.** And the track refills **only when the
  game tells you to** — at the end of *Griefbound Step IV*, or when an Opportunity card says to.
  `[RB p.19]`
- **Why people get it wrong:** "Next Turn" reads like "not yet available", and groups instinctively refill the
  moment a card dies. Refilling early quietly wrecks your Final Act timing.
- **Receipts:** 10p ([3629048](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3629048)) +
  8p/2r ([3617871](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3617871)).
- **Why it matters:** killing the *This Turn* card triggers the Opportunity roll, which **usually** refills —
  and refilling is what can tip you into the Final Act. Deliberately killing the *Next Turn* card instead is a
  real tactic for controlling when the endgame starts.
- **Errata note:** the Devourer's "Break" and "Exchange" Opportunity cards are missing the refill instruction.
  **Always refill after an Opportunity effect unless the card says otherwise.** `[ERR p.1]`
- **Clip caption:** *"Refilling the track early is how you lose the Final Act before it starts."*

## 4. Forced movement: do you still grab the loot you land on?

**← the honest "unresolved" one.**

- **Short answer:** **Officially unclear.** The rulebook ties pickup to ending *an Action*: "When you end your
  Action, including Movement, on a space with a Lament token or a Treasure, you **may** gain it. If there is a
  **Status** token, you **have to** gain it." `[RB p.12]` Forced movement from an Event, Corruption card or
  Enemy isn't your Action — so by a strict reading, no pickup. Many groups play that you do.
- **What *is* settled:** `[FAQ p.1]`
  - "On enter" fires **only if a model ENDS its movement** there — never for passing through. `[ERR p.1]`
    confirms the rulebook over the tutorial booklet.
  - **On enter DOES trigger on teleports and on forced movement.**
  - It does **not** re-trigger if you were already standing there.
  - Voluntary movement can be shorter than stated; **forced movement must be resolved to the fullest extent
    possible.**
- **Also unresolved:** whether "on enter" resolves *before* or *after* pickup. A dev reportedly said it's left
  to your group — just be consistent.
- **Receipts:** 15p / 1r — [3607499](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3607499).
- **Clip caption:** *"The rulebook answers 'on enter'. It does NOT answer this one — pick a house ruling."*

## 5. Keeper's Hunger Call: where does the "or" actually split?

**← keep the juiciest one near the end.**

- **The question:** the tile reads *"Gain 1 Craving **or** Move 1 and resolve the attack."* Is it
  **(Gain Craving or Move 1)** *then* attack — or **(Gain Craving)** *or* **(Move 1 and attack)**?
- **Short answer:** **genuinely ambiguous and not officially resolved.** The thread splits hard, with
  confident posters on both sides. Note the two readings differ by a whole attack, every single round.
- **Why it's a mess:** the **Corrupted** side of the tile reads *"Gain 1, Move 1, **and** resolve"* — all
  three. So the uncorrupted side's "or" is doing load-bearing work that the punctuation doesn't support.
  As one player put it: *"clear as mud."*
- **Receipts:** 15p — [3630755](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3630755). The Keeper spawns repeat trouble:
  see also "Infinite Hunger Glitch" (15p, [3634913](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3634913)).
- **Clip caption:** *"Two readings. One costs you an attack every round. Nobody official has answered."*

## Honorable mentions (quick-hit FAQ)

- **Technomancer's "all power-ups are now active" does NOT trigger them.** It *enables* them to be activated
  later by other Weapon/Armor Actions. `[FAQ p.2]` — 13p,
  [3631605](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3631605)
- **An Action cannot Refresh itself.** *Hunger Overflow*'s "Refresh 1 Action" can't pick itself. `[FAQ p.1]`
- **Blocked damage isn't dealt.** Defense-blocked damage discards **no** Hunger, and if you only had 3 Health
  left you only dealt 3 — so only 3 Hunger goes. `[FAQ p.1]`
- **Only one Status token per Action.** No stacking Stuns on the same Action. `[FAQ p.1]`
- **Out of Status/Hunger tokens? Take 1 damage per token you can't gain** — never substitute components.
  `[RB p.11]`, `[RB p.22]`
- **Burn distribution order matters when the pool runs dry.** Roll each attack per hunter separately, applying
  damage and tokens hunter by hunter. 12p, [3676898](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3676898)
- **Death Jester's hand survives between turns** — but you **discard your whole hand when you Pass**, and
  that's when the Fate discard reshuffles. There's no hand limit. `[RB p.29]` — 34p,
  [3618776](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3618776)
- **Mannequin: flipping a Personality token does NOT unblock the blocked Basic Action** — it stays blocked
  until you pick a new Personality in the Refresh step. Community reading; not stated explicitly. 12p,
  [3669618](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3669618)
- **Corruption 20 is just a ceiling** — nothing special fires, you simply can't advance further (and can still
  be moved back down, then re-evolve). Community consensus, no rulebook line. 9p/1r,
  [3615659](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3615659)
- **⚠️ Don't trust the "Ludomentor" AI rules assistant.** This warning recurs across at least three threads,
  including a dev quoted as saying the AI *"has delusions about how the game works all the time."* Verify
  against the rulebook or the FAQ.

# PART 2 — Top 3 Homebrew Rules

> **Caveat up front:** Grimcoven's Variants forum has only **7 threads** — an unusually thin homebrew scene
> for a game with 236 Rules threads. The community's energy is going into *asking what the rules are*, not
> rewriting them. So the picks below pull the balance discussion from the Rules forum too, where the real
> house-ruling is happening. Treat this as a smaller, more honest list rather than a padded top-10.

## 1. Nerf the Empress — "on a successful Gamble (**limit once per Action**)"

**Fixes: the Death Jester infinite-combo that trivializes bosses.**

- **The rule:** cap *Empress*'s passive at **once per Action** instead of once per successful Gamble. Some
  groups allow twice. Pair it with a nerf to the Jester's unlockable *Marotte* weapon, which is what enables
  the chain in the first place.
- **The why:** with Marotte + Empress you draw Fate cards off your own successes, which fuels more successes —
  players report chains long enough to erase a Griefbound outright. One player: *"you pretty much won the
  game."* Even post-nerf the card is rated top-tier, so this costs you nothing but the degenerate line.
  h/t **Vathar** and **DarthVerder** on BGG.
- **Receipts:** proposed in Variants 5p ([3636148](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3636148)), argued out in
  Rules 25p/1r ([3633347](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3633347)) — **the only balance topic that recurs
  across both forums.**
- **Status:** the publisher's official ruling (see Part 1 #1) **already defuses the infinite damage** by
  making the draw effects resolve last. This house rule goes further and clips the card's power level itself.
- **Clip caption:** *"The devs killed the infinite. The community still thinks she's too strong."*

## 2. Play solo three-handed — not two hunters, and not the Deputy

**Fixes: solo feeling harder than a real 3-player game.**

- **The rule:** run **3 full hunters** yourself instead of the rulebook's hunter + Deputy, or the common
  2-hunter solo. Note the game already scales to you: Griefbound and Elite Level = hunters **− 1**, so a 3rd
  hunter raises the difficulty knob too — this is not a free win. `[RB p.8-9]`
- **The why:** *"That 3rd hunter really helps control the field."* Two hunters lack the board presence and
  the Assist-marker rotation that makes symbol requirements manageable. Multiple regulars report 3-handed is
  barely more mental load than 2-handed and **more fun**; the OP tried it and reported back:
  *"it was great… I feel much more in control."* The real cost is **table space**, not brainpower.
  h/t **Bambooty**, **Vathar** on BGG.
- **Receipts:** 10p — [3681253](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3681253) — the most-discussed Variants thread.
- **Clip caption:** *"Solo Grimcoven is easier with MORE hunters. Play three."*

## 3. "Grimcoven Redux" — fix the Companions nobody uses

**← end on the most popular. Fixes: dead cards and fiddly components.**

- **The rule:** a free fan-made card pack that reworks the **Hunting Owl**'s activation and bumps the **Dog**'s
  range to **0–1**, plus redesigned *Marotte* and its combo Evolution card, a redone Hunt Sheet, and
  thinner printable Lament Rift tokens.
- **The why:** the author's framing — the Companions were *"completely useless during hunts"* — and the
  official cardboard Rift tokens *"took up half a hex, which caused models placed on them to wobble."* On the
  Marotte: *"My personal record is 21 successful Gamblings, and I didn't even have a character built
  specifically for Gambling."* h/t **Johnnyw85** on BGG.
- **Receipts:** **11 recommends** — by far the most-endorsed variant in the forum, and it earned them on a
  single post — [3743040](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3743040).
- **Nice corroboration:** the Hunting Owl was *also* broken enough to need **official errata** — its card is
  missing the "do not discard `[Universal Marker]` from this card" line, without which the Owl is useless.
  `[ERR p.1]` So the community and the publisher independently flagged the same card.
- **Clip caption:** *"When the publisher errata's the same card your fan-fix targets, you know it was broken."*

## Swap-ins (honorable mentions)

- **Abandon the 2-action turn structure** — go free-form, players act when they want. Proposed to kill the
  *"talking, debating and dare I say it: alpha gaming"* that adds 1–2 hours to a game. **Contested:** replies
  pushed back hard, arguing the 2-action cap is precisely what *stops* AP players from spiraling. Good clip
  fodder as a debate, not as a recommendation. 5p/1r —
  [3718037](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3718037)
- **True solo, one hunter, no Deputy** — the purist's ask. **Community verdict: don't.** *"The entire game is
  designed on having two characters"* — you lose Assist entirely and would have to rewrite a big chunk. Also
  note the Deputy counts as a Hunter for `[#H]` and **if the Deputy dies you lose.** `[RB p.30]` 4p —
  [3646687](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3646687)
- **Strip duplicate Evolution cards from hunter decks** — proposed to stop e.g. the Keeper's doubled *Hunger
  Overflow*. **Community rejected it:** the dupes aren't the problem, *"the Empress is the only [card] that
  could use a nerf."* Included as the variant the forum talked itself out of. 5p —
  [3636148](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3636148)
- **RPGrief** — a custom beginner-friendly weapon card by the Redux author. A component file rather than a
  rules change. 3p/2r — [3737517](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3737517)
